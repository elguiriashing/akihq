# AkiHQ suite controls — release candidate, 23 September 2026

This branch is a substantial control improvement, **not approval to market AkiHQ as a fully compliant invoicing, accounting or payroll suite**. See [the requirements matrix](SUITE-COMPLIANCE.md). The initial fiscal preparation scope is Spanish common territory, general IVA, EUR and businesses outside SII. Foral territories, IGIC/IPSI, special IVA regimes and other countries need their own implementations.

## What is implemented

- Server-authoritative checkout prices, exact money, operator validation, stable retry identity, cross-tab coordination and atomic operational stock deduction.
- Receipts with supplier references and net costs, locations/transfers, manager counts/waste, immutable movement/value evidence, explicit unknown historical costs and historical weighted-average exports.
- Fiscal issuer/IVA setup, an immutable numbered-document/correction model, period locks and complete reconciled Excel/JSON ledger exports. **Issuance is hard-disabled**; existing operational sales do not acquire guessed historic IVA.
- AEAT XML/hash/QR primitives checked against official examples/schemas; the September 25 adapter now chains issuance atomically and supplies Node mTLS transport primitives, but no deployed dispatcher or validated customer invoice is present.
- Membership/module/export permissions, filtered optimistic-lock snapshots, safe cashier catalogue, profile escalation prevention, secret redaction and protected server audit.
- Controller settings, consent/suppression evidence, rights tracking, partial subject export, gated marketing delivery/unsubscribe/provider callbacks and AI disclosure on AI support replies.
- Business controls UI, pending transaction recovery, snapshot conflict recovery, scoped browser caches and truthful integration/module status.

## Acceptance gates

Run `npm ci --prefix cloudflare` and `npm --prefix cloudflare run check`. GitHub Actions runs the same check without production credentials. Build the Worker with `cd cloudflare && npx wrangler deploy --dry-run`.

Staging acceptance must exercise separate authenticated owner, cashier, restricted staff, read-only and outsider accounts; two tenants; two browser tabs; two independent database sessions; expired access; logout/workspace change during a delayed response; uncertain commit/retry; item price changes; Main stock versus a secondary location; stale counts; cancellation tombstones; permissions revoked with unsaved local edits; large exports and browser downloads. PGlite and jsdom cover executable behavior but are not substitutes for that acceptance.

Verify operational totals and every exported sheet against the database. Verify upload/attachment isolation, provider callback failures, approved sender ownership and actual backup/restore. Keep marketing off until the real sender, public unsubscribe origin and signed provider webhook are configured and exercised. See [privacy operations](PRIVACY-OPERATIONS.md).

## Fiscal activation is a separate release

The gate cannot be unlocked merely by marking the release registry approved. The September 25 migration implements atomic fiscal issue/chain/outbox and a gated full-payment sale/stock adapter. Missing work includes connecting hospitality final/split payments to that adapter; real AEAT authentication and representation; retries and acknowledgement/rejection handling; complete invoice rendering/QR validation; and tested recovery with chain continuity. The current candidate does not satisfy all these steps.

The actual producer must prepare and incorporate the version-specific declaration of responsibility for the completed system. AEAT describes producer self-certification, not an external “AEAT approved” badge. Business fiscal identities, product classifications and authority to submit must be real and checked. B2B structured electronic invoicing is an additional implementation, not supplied by a PDF or the VERI*FACTU XML primitive.

## Wider suite work still required

The matrix distinguishes legal obligations from competitor features. Remaining scope includes complete GDPR rights/erasure coverage and retention execution across modules/files/providers; actual processing agreements, subprocessors and incident procedures; privileged-account MFA/password protection; verified restore/exit exports; public-site privacy/cookie/accessibility and consumer flows where applicable; statutory working-time records if offered; payroll/social-security and sector rules if offered; and accounting journal/bank/payment reconciliation and specialized stock traceability as supported scope requires. The People, Sites/Forms and Automation records must not be advertised as services they do not implement.

Existing overly broad access warrants an evidence-based incident assessment. This review did not establish that a disclosure or breach occurred. Rotate only credentials actually implicated by that assessment; never publish secrets or customer records in issue/PR logs.
